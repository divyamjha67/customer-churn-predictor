Evaluation details & limits

Separate decisions

Median imputation, scaling, encoding, and the classifier travel together in one fitted pipeline. Gender, senior status, partner, and dependents are excluded; this does not establish fairness.

A benchmark, with boundaries

This is IBM’s fictional telecom sample. There is no timestamped history for a future-time evaluation. Scores estimate the dataset’s churn label; they are not validated next-month forecasts or guarantees.

No intervention experiment is available. The workbench makes no claims about savings, retention uplift, or causal reasons.
